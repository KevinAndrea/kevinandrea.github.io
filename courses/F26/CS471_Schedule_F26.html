Test and Homework Schedule

Tests are bigger milestones, and their dates are announced ahead of time.

Only severe scheduling anomalies would force a delay in test dates.

  • Homeworks:
    • Each Homework Releases on Wednesday and is due on Friday by 11:59pm on the following week.
    • These week-long homework assignments will be done on Gradescope.
    • There are no makeups for a missed or late homework assignments.

  • Mid-Term Exams:
    • Test 1: Thursday, October 15th
      • Covers Modules 1 - 4
        • OS Structures and Interrupt Handling
        • Processes, Signals, and IPC
        • Threads and Synchronization
    • Test 2: Thursday, November 19th
      • Covers Modules 5 - 6
        • CPU Scheduling
        • Memory Management
    • More information will be provided as the date closes in.
    • The exams will given in-person during your normal class session.

  • Final Exam: See Exam Schedule for the Date and Times.
    • The Final Exam is Comprehensive
    • https://registrar.gmu.edu/fall-2026-final-exam-schedule/
      • Section 003 with Prof. Andrea
        • Tuesday, Dec 15th from 10:30am - 1:15pm
      • Section 004 with Prof. Andrea
        • Thursday, Dec 10th from 10:30am - 1:15pm
    • The Final will be taken during these University Scheduled Times in your normal classroom.
